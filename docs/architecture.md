## Overview

Word Sort is Moodle Activity Module (mod) designed to provide a timed categorization activity.

The plugin consists of three main parts:

- Teacher interface
- Student interface
- Reporting interface

---

## Teacher interface

The teacher creates a new activity and configures:

- Activity name
- Category 1
- Category 2
- Time limit
- Maximum attempts
- Shuffle itmes
- Immediate feedback

The teacher then adds words or phrases and assigns the correct category.

---

## Student Interface

The student opens the activity and starts and attempt.

During the attempt:

- one prompt is shown at a time
- the timer counts up
- the student selects one of two categories
- the next prompt appears automatically

The attempt ends when all prompts are answered.

--- 

## Reporting

Teachers can review:

- attempts
- scores
- time used
- individual answers
