## Overview

Word Sort is Moodle Activity Module (mod) designed to provide a timed categorization activity.

Moto: Keep the activity simple for teachers to set up, but powerful enough to support different teaching styles.

The plugin consists of three main parts:

- Teacher interface
- Student interface
- Reporting interface

---

## Teacher interface

The teacher creates a new activity and configures:

- Activity name
- Category 1
- Category 2
- Timing mode (seconds)
    ○ No timer
    ○ Countdown
        Student has a fixed amount of time.
    ○ Stopwatch
        Time counts up until the student finishes.
- Maximum attempts
- Shuffle itmes
- Immediate feedback

The teacher then adds words or phrases and assigns the correct category.

---

## Student Interface

The student opens the activity and starts and attempt.

During the attempt:

- one prompt is shown at a time
- the timer counts up
- the student selects one of two categories
- the next prompt appears automatically

The attempt ends when all prompts are answered.

--- 

## Reporting

Teachers can review:

- attempts
- scores
- time used
- individual answers

Scoring

○ Correct answers only
○ Correct answers + completion time
○ Time bonus

## Attempts and grading

Students may retry the activity until they submit their result or until the maximum number of attempts has been reached. Practice attempts are not stored in the gradebook. When the result is submitted, the plugin records only the best attempt achieved during that session.
